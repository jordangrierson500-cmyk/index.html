Mandible AR

A 3D mandible that stands on your desk card. Spin it, resize it and switch between the growth features from our presentation.

  1. Lay your desk card flat, with the ▼ edge toward you.
  2. Tap Start camera and allow the camera.
  3. Point your phone at the card. The jaw appears on the circle.
  4. Drag to spin, pinch to resize, double-tap to reset. Pick a feature at the bottom.

·

Works in Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android. Everything runs on your phone; no video is uploaded. The A4 mat from the front of the room works too.
